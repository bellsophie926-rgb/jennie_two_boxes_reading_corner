The Two Boxes in the Reading Corner

A picture-book style reading page for Jennie. 重点反复重现：所有格、双重所有格、forget to do、late 的正确用法、one...the other、some...others。

Before Reading｜先看 late 的用法

late 不是普通动词。所以不能写 He didn’t late.

Correct Patterns 正确句型

He was late. = 他迟到了。

He wasn’t late. = 他没有迟到。

He arrived late. = 他到晚了。

He didn’t arrive late. = 他没有到晚。

He didn’t late. = 错，因为 late 不是谓语动词。

pink = ’s / s’ 所有格green = of 所有格purple = 双重所有格orange = forget to dored = late 正确用法blue = one...the other...green = some...others...purple = keep sb doing

After Reading｜读后小练习

A. Choose the correct sentence.

  1. A. He didn’t late.
    B. He wasn’t late.
  2. A. He didn’t arrive late.
    B. He didn’t arrived late.
  3. A. One form records the book name, and the other records the borrower’s name.
    B. One form records the book name, and other records the borrower’s name.
  4. A. Some students read, and others help.
    B. Some students read, and the other help.

B. Fill in the blanks.

  1. Don’t forget ______ write your name.
  2. The door ______ the classroom is open.
  3. The teachers______ office is next door.
  4. This is a notebook ______ Lily’s.
  5. Tom found his card ______ time, so he wasn’t late.
  6. Please return the book ______ time.
Answer Key｜答案

A: 1-B, 2-A, 3-A, 4-A

B: 1. to 2. of 3. ’ 4. of 5. in 6. on
